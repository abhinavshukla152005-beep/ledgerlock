Expense policy for FY27, effective 1 October 2026.

All amounts are in INR. Meal receipts are capped at 1,500 per transaction, and total meal spend per person per day may not exceed 2,500. Hotel stays are capped at 6,000 per booking. Software and SaaS subscriptions are capped at 2,500 per transaction. Ground transport and flight change fees are capped at 800 per transaction.

Any expense above 1,000 must have a receipt attached. Any expense above 5,000 requires a named approver before reimbursement. If the same vendor charges the same amount more than once within 7 days, treat it as a suspected duplicate and hold it.
